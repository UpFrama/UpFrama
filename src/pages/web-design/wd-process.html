How a project runs

From first call to launch.

A simple process, with you seeing the work before anything goes live.

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    Goals, customers and content

    We learn about your business, your customers and what you want the site to do, then agree on the pages and features.

  2. 2

    Design preview

    Layout, look and wording

    You see the layout, colours and wording before we build, and we adjust it with you until it feels right.

  3. 3

    Build & connect

    Pages, forms and booking

    We build the site, add your booking and enquiry forms, and connect your AI voice agent if you use one.

  4. 4

    Launch & care

    Hosting and updates

    We publish the site, host it and keep it updated, and make changes when you need them.
