Call flows tuned to your trade.

Home Services & Trades

HVAC · Plumbing · Electrical · Roofing

Triage
Sorts each call into emergency, same-day or routine from what the caller describes.
Dispatch
Texts the on-call technician the address, the problem and the callback number.
Booking
Offers slots by service area, job type and crew availability.
CRM
Creates the job in ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro or Jobber.

Healthcare & Wellness Clinics

Dental · Medical · Wellness

Triage
Separates urgent requests from routine scheduling and passes anything clinical to your staff.
Dispatch
Routes urgent callers to the on-call line or clinician by SMS.
Booking
Books and reschedules by provider, visit type and open slots.
CRM
Logs the call and the appointment to HubSpot or your CRM through a webhook.

Professional Services

Legal · Accounting · Real Estate

Triage
Qualifies each new inquiry by matter type, deadline and urgency.
Dispatch
Alerts the right attorney, accountant or agent when a lead is time-sensitive.
Booking
Books consultations on the right person's calendar in Cal.com, Google Calendar or Calendly.
CRM
Creates the contact and intake notes in HubSpot.

Automotive Repair & Fleet

Repair shops · Fleet services

Triage
Tells a drivable car from a breakdown and records make, model and symptoms.
Dispatch
Notifies the service advisor or mobile team for roadside and fleet emergencies.
Booking

CRM
