A closer look at six websites we have designed, and the thinking behind each one.

01 / 06

Sonic Alchemy

Music producer website

  • Website
  • Mobile-first
  • Typography

A bold, dark website for a music producer working between New York and London. A short, confident headline leads into a clear breakdown of production, mixing, mastering and recording, followed by key numbers, recent work and an FAQ that answers common questions before a client gets in touch.

What we focused on

  • Large stencil-style type for a strong first impression
  • Phone-first layout with a glass navigation bar and one clear action
  • Services, numbers, work and FAQ in one easy scroll

02 / 06

Pitchcasck

AI tool landing page

  • Landing page
  • Branding feel

A landing page for an AI tool that analyses pitch decks in minutes. The whole idea is explained with two cards, upload your deck and get a complete report, and everything else on the page supports that single action.

What we focused on

  • One idea, two steps, shown visually
  • Soft gradient background with a classic serif headline
  • The main action repeated in the header and the hero

03 / 06

Flowline

SaaS website

  • SaaS
  • Dashboard UI
  • Product site

A dark product website for a platform that brings a team's tools together. The hero pairs a clear headline with a dashboard preview, then moves through features, a simple process, pricing and FAQ, with a strip of integrations showing the tools it connects to.

What we focused on

  • A dashboard preview that shows the product at a glance
  • Orange kept for actions and key numbers only
  • Stats and an integrations row for quick context

04 / 06

Clean UI

Product landing page

  • Landing page
  • Light theme

A light, airy landing page for a data tool, built around a single prompt box in front of a soft green landscape. The headline says what the product does and the input shows how to start.

What we focused on

  • Generous whitespace and one strong headline
  • A glass input panel as the main interaction
  • A calm natural background that keeps the page friendly

05 / 06

Prelude Neo

Product launch page

  • Launch page
  • Editorial

A launch page for a new car that places the new model beside the original that inspired it. An oversized headline sits between the two, with a reserve button and a specs link kept simple underneath.

What we focused on

  • Photography does most of the talking
  • Oversized type layered behind the cars

06 / 06

Forma Studio

Architecture studio website

  • Studio site
  • Editorial
  • Portfolio

A refined website for an architecture and design studio, led by large serif type over night-time photography. Project, country and year counts sit quietly at the bottom of the first screen, and an editorial philosophy section follows.

What we focused on

  • Serif display type paired with small-caps navigation
  • Dark, moody imagery with warm orange accents
  • Editorial layouts that make room for architectural drawings
