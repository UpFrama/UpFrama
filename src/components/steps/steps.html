How it works

Live in four steps.

We handle the build, the testing and the cutover.

  1. 1

    Phone Line Audit & Script Architecture

    Custom speech persona & knowledge base

    We review your call patterns, services and booking rules, then write the voice and the knowledge your agent speaks from.

  2. 2

    CRM & Calendar Integration

    Live webhook & API pairing

    The agent is paired with your calendar and CRM so bookings and job records are written as the call happens.

  3. 3

    Test Flight On Real Numbers

    Edge-case and objection handling

    We test awkward callers, odd requests and objections on real phone numbers before go-live.

  4. 4

    Go Live & Scale

    Call forwarding and scaling

    Calls are forwarded to the agent, and capacity grows with your call volume.
