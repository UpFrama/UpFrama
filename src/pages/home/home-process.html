How we work

From first call to live in four steps.

The same simple process applies whether we are setting up a voice agent, a website or both.

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    15 minutes, no obligation

    We learn how your business runs, where calls or enquiries get missed and what a good result looks like.

  2. 2

    Plan and build

    Scripts, pages and integrations

    We write the agent's knowledge or the site's pages and connect your calendar, CRM and contact details.

  3. 3

    Test with you

    Real numbers and real devices

    You review it, and we test awkward callers, odd requests and small screens before anything goes live.

  4. 4

    Launch and support

    Go live, then keep improving

    We switch it on, watch how it performs and make changes as your business changes.
